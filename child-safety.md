# NeighborLoop Child Safety Standards

**Effective Date: October 14, 2026**
**Last Updated: October 14, 2026**

NeighborLoop is committed to maintaining a safe community for all users. NeighborLoop has zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE), child sexual abuse material (CSAM), grooming, sexual exploitation of minors, or other conduct that puts children at risk.

## Prohibited Content and Conduct

NeighborLoop prohibits:

- Any sexual content involving minors.
- Child sexual abuse material (CSAM).
- Sexual exploitation or solicitation of minors.
- Grooming or attempts to establish inappropriate sexual relationships with minors.
- Requests for, offers of, or distribution of sexual images involving minors.
- Any other conduct involving the sexual abuse or exploitation of children.

## Reporting Child Safety Concerns

Users can report safety concerns through the reporting functionality available within NeighborLoop.

Reports involving suspected child sexual abuse or exploitation are treated as a priority and may result in removal of content, restriction or termination of accounts, and other appropriate action.

Child safety concerns may also be reported to:

**support@neighboorloop.com**

## Enforcement

NeighborLoop may remove prohibited content and suspend or terminate accounts involved in CSAE or other serious child-safety violations.

NeighborLoop may preserve relevant information and cooperate with law enforcement or appropriate authorities when required by applicable law.

## Legal Compliance

NeighborLoop complies with applicable child-safety and child sexual exploitation laws and regulations and will make required reports to appropriate regional or national authorities.

## Child Safety Contact

For questions or concerns regarding child safety, suspected CSAM, or CSAE prevention and compliance, contact:

**support@neighboorloop.com**
