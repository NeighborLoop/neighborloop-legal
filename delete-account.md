# Delete Your NeighborLoop Account

If you would like to delete your NeighborLoop account and associated personal data, you can request account deletion through NeighborLoop Support.

## How to Request Account Deletion

1. Visit the NeighborLoop website.
2. Use the Support form to submit an account deletion request.
3. Include the email address associated with your NeighborLoop account.
4. State that you are requesting deletion of your NeighborLoop account.

You do not need to provide your password.

## What Happens When You Request Deletion

We will process your request in accordance with the NeighborLoop Privacy Policy and applicable requirements.

Account information and personal data associated with your NeighborLoop account will be deleted as described in our Privacy Policy, subject to information that may need to be retained for legal, security, fraud-prevention, or other legitimate purposes.

## Need Help?

If you have questions about account deletion or your personal data, please contact NeighborLoop Support through the NeighborLoop website.

## Privacy Policy

For more information about how NeighborLoop collects, uses, and protects information, please review our Privacy Policy.
